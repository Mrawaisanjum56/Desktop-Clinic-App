Put your clinic logo image here as:

    logo.png

Recommended: a roughly square PNG (e.g. 300x300px), transparent background
looks best. It will automatically appear:
  - on the login screen (next to the clinic name)
  - on the top-left of every prescription and bill PDF

If no logo.png is present, the app falls back to showing the clinic's
initials in a colored circle instead — nothing breaks either way.

You can change the expected path/filename in clinic_config.json
("clinic_logo_path"), if you'd rather use a different name or location.
